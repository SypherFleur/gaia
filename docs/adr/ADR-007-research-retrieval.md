# ADR-007: Research Retrieval

Status: Accepted

## Context

Scholar must support research-grade synthesis without treating every source as equal or violating licensing.

## Decision

Research retrieval separates peer-reviewed literature, government guidance, extension material, community experience, and experimental signals. Source licenses and rights are recorded before storage, redistribution, caching, or training use.

## Consequences

- Public accessibility does not imply model-training rights.
- Contradictory evidence must be represented.
- Complex institutional RAG is deferred until provenance is stable.
