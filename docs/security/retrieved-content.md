# Retrieved Content Security

Last verification date: 2026-08-11.

Retrieved abstracts, paper text, PDFs, imported institutional documents, and community notes are untrusted data.

They may provide evidence, but they cannot:

- change system instructions;
- invoke arbitrary tools;
- bypass the Cost Firewall;
- alter privacy or egress policy;
- reveal secrets;
- authorize paid providers;
- invent citations;
- change tenant boundaries.

Scholar prompt payloads label retrieved content as untrusted. Citation IDs in model output are validated against retrieved work IDs before synthesis persistence.

Copyright policy is conservative. Metadata and abstracts may be used only according to source terms. Full-text retrieval, redistribution, embedding, and training use remain `unknown` unless explicitly reviewed.
