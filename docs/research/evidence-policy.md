# Evidence Policy

Last verification date: 2026-08-11.

## Evidence Grades

- `A`: direct measurement or local observation plus authoritative current data.
- `B`: strong authoritative government guidance or high-quality peer-reviewed evidence.
- `C`: established agronomic/horticultural reference or moderate peer-reviewed evidence.
- `D`: community/grower experiential knowledge.
- `E`: preliminary research, experimental signal, traditional practice, weak evidence, or retracted/irrelevant evidence.

Evidence grade is not model confidence. Scholar stores evidence quality, model confidence, and data freshness separately.

## Evidence Direction

Research claims are classified as supporting, contradictory, uncertain, or irrelevant. Contradictory evidence remains visible in synthesis.

## Study Type

Scholar preserves study type when available or inferable: meta-analysis, systematic review, randomized controlled trial, controlled experiment, observational study, field trial, laboratory study, review, case study, expert opinion, or unknown.

## Applicability

Applicability dimensions include species, cultivar, growing system, climate, geography, soil, development stage, and intervention match. A greenhouse result is not automatically universal outdoor guidance.

## Retractions

Retracted work is retained for audit/provenance but should not be used as normal supporting evidence.

## Citations

A valid citation must match retrieved work identity: DOI, PMID, PMCID, Europe PMC/provider ID, or GAIA ResearchWork ID. Model-generated unknown citations are rejected.
